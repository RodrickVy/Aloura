Section 1

Why this experiment

We have a working MVP. The product already does the hard part — search outfits, compare prices across 12+ stores, upload a look and shop it, trending outfits, and color/style matching. The unknown now is demand: will people sign up, use it, and tell us it's useful?

This experiment answers that cheaply, on a fixed budget and a fixed timeline, before spending more on the product or on ads.

Success = 100 sign-ups + 50 feedback submissions in 2 weeks

Section 3

The campaigns

All run at the same time and share the same goal: drive a click → a moodboard → a sign-up → feedback. Every post ends with a clear call to action and the Linktree link.

Campaign 1 — World Cup Jerseys

  • Idea: One post per national team. Show the team's kit moodboard (jersey + shorts) and link to it.
  • Why it works: The World Cup is a massive, timely, searched-for topic. People are emotionally attached to their team and actively looking for kits.
  • Execution: Pre-generate a moodboard for each team. Each post = team colors/kit visual + "Shop [Country]'s 2026 kit → link in bio." Linktree has a tidy list of teams.
  • Volume: This alone can carry many of the 42 posts (there are 48 teams).

Campaign 2 — Trending Outfit Recreations

  • Idea: Showcase a currently-trending look (streetwear, old money, date night, etc.), then link to a moodboard that lets people recreate and shop it.
  • Why it works: "Recreate this outfit / where do I buy this look" is one of the most shared fashion formats online.
  • Execution: Short Reels/Shorts: "This look is everywhere right now — here's how to get it for less," ending on the moodboard link. Reuse on YouTube Shorts and Pinterest.

Campaign 3 — Share Your Outfit Ideas

  • Idea: Community-driven. Invite people to recreate a look, or post "outfit idea → shoppable board" content that encourages sharing and saving.
  • Why it works: Turns viewers into participants and creates word-of-mouth. Every shared Aloura link is a free referral with a proper preview image.
  • Execution: Lighter, faster posts. Polls, "rate this fit," "we built a board for this — would you wear it?"

Vector 4 — Influencer / creator partnerships

  • Idea: Reach out to fashion/styling creators. Offer to build them a moodboard for their outfit videos so their followers can actually buy the items they talk about — one clean link instead of "everything's in my bio."
  • The future hook: soon they'll get their own creator page to add products and earn through commission links — so they benefit by getting in early on a growing platform.
  • Why it works: Creators constantly get "where's that from?" Aloura answers it. Their audience is exactly our target shopper. One creator post can outperform dozens of ours.
  • Execution: Personalized DMs/emails (see the separate Outreach Scripts document), starting with smaller/mid creators. We build the board for them — zero effort on their side.
Section 4

Posting plan

  • Cadence: 3 posts/day × 14 days = 42 posts.
  • Split (guideline): ~50% World Cup, ~30% Trending Recreations, ~20% Share Ideas — adjust based on traction.
  • Channels: Instagram (primary: Reels + carousels), YouTube Shorts (repurpose), Pinterest (pin the boards for long-tail search traffic).
  • Every post: strong visual + 1 sentence hook + "link in bio" → Linktree → moodboard.
  • Tracking: Watch the in-app analytics (sign-ups, onboarded, searches, comparisons, trends, shares, buy clicks) daily to see which campaign converts.

Section 9

Things to decide on

These are open choices — we should agree before launch:

  1. Cold calling / cold outreach — yes or no? Posting is the baseline. Do we also do direct outreach (DMs, communities, emails)? It can boost sign-ups but takes time.
  2. Do we build a social engagement presence, or purely post-and-link? (Replying to comments, following, community-building drives more growth but is more work — does the hired poster do this, and how much?)
  3. SerpAPI plan: start on $75 Developer (recommended) or risk the $25 plan and upgrade if we run out?
  4. Claude budget cap for the campaign — set a hard monthly ceiling so a traffic spike can't run up an unexpected bill.
  5. Poster scope & pay structure — flat $250 for the run, or hourly? Posting only vs. posting + engagement?
  6. Paid ads — not now or later? Recommendation: stay organic for this experiment; only consider paid after we see the funnel convert.
  7. Channel priority — all of Instagram/YouTube/Pinterest, or focus on Instagram first?

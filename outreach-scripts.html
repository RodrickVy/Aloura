Outreach Scripts

How to use this

One goal, three audiences

Our target for this experiment: 100 sign-ups and at least 50 feedback responses. Every message below is written to lead someone to try Aloura and then leave quick feedback.

  • Always reference the feedback button — it sits at the bottom of every page. Tell people exactly where it is so they don't have to hunt for it.
  • Keep it short and human. The shorter scripts usually get more replies — start there.
  • Personalize the first line (their name, their video, something specific). Never paste cold.
  • The site: joinaloura.com  ·  IG: @_joinaloura_  ·  Email: shopaloura.info@gmail.com
Audience 1

Influencers & creators

For fashion/styling creators who post "how I style this" and outfit videos.

The pitch in one line: "We'll build you a moodboard so your followers can actually buy the items from your outfit videos — and soon you'll get your own page to add products and earn commission. Get in early while we're growing."

Short — first DM (Instagram)

InfluencerShort · DM

Hey [Name] — love your styling videos. I run Aloura, a new app that turns any outfit into a shoppable list across 20+ stores. Would you be open to us building you a free moodboard for one of your looks, so your followers can actually buy the pieces from one link? Early creators will also get their own page to add products and earn commission as we grow. Happy to show you.

Medium — DM / email

InfluencerMedium

Hi [Name], I'm Rodrick, co-founder of Aloura (joinaloura.com). We help people shop the outfits they see online — upload or describe a look and we find the pieces across 20+ stores and compare prices. Your audience is exactly who we're built for. I'd love to create a free moodboard for one of your outfit videos so your followers can buy the items in one tap instead of digging through your bio. Aloura is early but growing fast, and we're bringing creators in now: soon you'll have your own page where you add products and earn through commission links. Getting in early means more reach and a head start on that. No work on your side — we'll build the board for you. Want me to put one together for your last look so you can see it?

Longer — warm email (for someone who replied)

InfluencerLong · Email

Hi [Name], Thanks for getting back to me — really appreciate it. Quick context: Aloura is a styling + shopping platform. People come to personalize their style and shop with confidence — they can search a vibe or upload a photo of an outfit, and we pull together the actual pieces from 20+ brands and compare prices so they get the best deal. Here's what I'd love to do with you: 1) I build a free, shoppable moodboard for one of your recent outfit videos — one clean link your followers can use to buy the pieces. 2) You share it however feels natural (bio link, story, pinned comment). 3) As we roll out creator pages, you'll be able to add your own products and earn commission on what your audience buys. We're early, which is the point — the creators who join now get the most visibility and the best terms as the platform grows. There's no cost and no real effort on your end; we do the building. Could I send you a sample board this week? If you tell me which look to use, I'll have it ready in a day. Thanks, Rodrick Vyizigiro Co-founder, Aloura — joinaloura.com

Don't forget

The feedback ask

Every feedback request must clearly point to the feedback button at the bottom of the page. People won't look for it — tell them exactly where it is and that it's only 3 quick questions. That single line is what turns a "tester" into a "feedback response," which is half our goal.

Reusable closing line: "When you're done, scroll to the bottom and tap the feedback button — it's just 3 quick questions and it genuinely helps us a lot."
